Is it legal?

Straight answers, before you buy

The question everyone asks first, in plain language.

Based on Criminal Code s.84(3)(d).

Do I need a PAL or licence?

No. A launcher that can't fire faster than 152.4 m/s or with more than 5.7 joules isn't a "firearm" under the Firearms Act. Byrna launchers sit under that line, so no PAL, permit or background check. You must be 18 or older to buy.

Can I carry it in public?

Carrying any weapon for self-defence has limits under Canadian law. Our team will explain what applies where you live.
