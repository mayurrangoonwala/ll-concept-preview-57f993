Is it legal?

Straight answers, before you buy.

The question everyone asks first. Here's what Canadian law says about the launchers we sell, in plain language.

Based on Criminal Code s.84(3)(d). Read the full explanation →

Do I need a firearms licence (PAL) to own a Byrna?

No. Under Criminal Code s.84(3)(d), a barrelled weapon that can't fire a projectile faster than 152.4 m/s or with more than 5.7 joules of energy isn't a "firearm" under the Firearms Act. Byrna launchers sit under that line, so no PAL, permit or background check is needed.

Can I buy one in every province?

Byrna launchers are legal to own in every province. Some items can't ship to certain places, and federal, provincial and municipal rules on carrying and use still apply. We'll walk you through them.

Can I carry it in public?

Carrying any weapon for self-defence has limits under Canadian law. Our team will explain what applies where you live.

How old do I have to be?

You must be 18 or older to buy from Less Lethal.
